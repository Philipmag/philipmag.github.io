# Church Bulletin Announcement
## Digital Guardians — Free Cybersecurity Training for Seniors

---

**FREE DIGITAL SAFETY TRAINING — SUNDAY, JUNE 28, 2026**

*Hosted by Digital Guardians | Cybersecurity for Seniors*
*Peterborough, Ontario*

---

Are you worried about online scams, suspicious emails, or keeping your personal information safe? **Digital Guardians** is a local nonprofit dedicated to helping seniors in Peterborough stay safe in the digital world — and we are coming to you with a **free, in-person training day on Sunday, June 28, 2026.**

Two sessions are available. You are welcome to attend one or both. Seating is limited to 20 participants per session, so early registration is encouraged.

---

### MORNING SESSION — Beginner Friendly
**10:00 AM – 12:00 PM**

This session is designed for those who are new to computers, smartphones, or the internet, or who simply want to build more confidence online. No prior experience is required — bring your questions!

**Program:**

| Time | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 10:00 AM | Welcome & Introduction to Digital Guardians |
| 10:15 AM | What Is a Scam? — Common Types Targeting Seniors |
| 10:45 AM | Live Demo: How to Spot a Phishing Email or Text |
| 11:15 AM | Password Safety Workshop — Creating Strong, Memorable Passwords |
| 11:45 AM | Open Q&A — Bring Your Questions! |
| 12:00 PM | Session Ends |

---

### AFTERNOON SESSION — Intermediate
**2:00 PM – 4:00 PM**

This session is for those who are comfortable with the basics and want to go deeper into protecting their privacy and finances online.

**Program:**

| Time | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 2:00 PM | Welcome & Overview |
| 2:10 PM | Social Media Privacy Settings — Facebook, Instagram & More |
| 2:40 PM | Safe Online Banking — What to Watch For |
| 3:10 PM | Understanding AI & Chatbots — What They Are and How to Use Them Safely |
| 3:35 PM | Protecting Your Personal Information Online |
| 3:50 PM | Q&A & Resource Handout |
| 4:00 PM | Session Ends |

---

### WHAT TO BRING

Bring your smartphone, tablet, or laptop if you have one — our volunteers will help you apply what you learn directly to your own device. If you do not have a device, that is perfectly fine; printed materials will be provided.

### ABOUT DIGITAL GUARDIANS

Digital Guardians is a Peterborough-based nonprofit organization whose mission is to empower seniors with the knowledge and tools to navigate the internet safely. Our volunteer trainers are experienced cybersecurity professionals who are passionate about helping our community. All sessions are free of charge and designed specifically for older adults.

*"You deserve to feel safe and confident online — and we are here to help."*
